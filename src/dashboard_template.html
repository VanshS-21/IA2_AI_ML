Model limits and rollout criteria

Delivered-only labels exclude cancellations and unresolved shipments. Both models use the same checkout features and train-only imputation/encoding. The model is selected by validation average precision, then its threshold maximizes validation F2 under a 20% alert-capacity assumption. Test workload may differ under drift.

Before rollout: validate event-time availability, calibrate scores on a later window, backtest multiple seasons, set intervention costs and capacity, then run a randomized pilot. Track precision, recall, workload, fairness across routes, late rate and customer outcomes.

Metric definitions and scope

Revenue = delivered merchandise GMV, excluding freight. It is neither platform net revenue nor profit. Orders and customers are delivered-order counts in the chosen scope. AOV = GMV / delivered orders. Review mean uses the latest answered review per order and excludes missing reviews. Late delivery = actual delivery timestamp after estimated timestamp, among delivered orders with valid timestamps. Repeat customers have at least two delivered orders in the selected scope.

RFM segments use the full-period snapshot and are descriptive. Recent means within 90 days. High value means customer GMV at or above the 75th percentile. Cohort-adjusted retention, statistical tests, model metrics and scenario recommendations stay fixed in the reproducible notebook.

Only states and purchase months filter the first three pages. Category and seller delivery counts use distinct memberships and are not additive across groups. Payment totals include freight. Data spans 2016–2018 and does not describe current Olist performance.
